BitLex - 1010 bitbox micro modded firmware

Custom firmware v0.1 for the 1010music bitbox micro, patched in your browser.

What it adds

  • Live Loop pads: a pad re-records itself every loop (1 bar, 2 bars, ...) in time with the clock and keeps playing the last pass while it records, replacing it or overdubbing on top.
  • Fidelity: the Interp setting gains vintage sampler modes (SP1200, SP-12, S950, MPC60, SP Raw) and sample-rate crunch from 16k down to 2k.
  • Filter: the pad filter becomes a 24 dB/oct state-variable filter that growls at high resonance, in place of the stock 12 dB one.

Full documentation

Install at your own risk. This is unofficial firmware, not made or supported by 1010music. Any custom firmware carries some risk of bricking your bitbox or losing projects. Keep your stock firmware file and back up your SD card first.
  1. Get the official bitbox micro firmware version 2.3.4 (MICRO.bin) from 1010music.
  2. Choose that file below. It stays on your computer: the patching happens in this page.
  3. Save the modded MICRO.bin, copy it to the root of the microSD card and install it like a 1010music firmware update. If your browser saves it as MICRO (1).bin, rename it to MICRO.bin first.

Going back to stock firmware

Back up your projects first (copy the whole microSD card to your computer). Projects saved with the modded firmware won't work with the stock firmware, so after rolling back you can only use projects saved before you installed the mod.
  1. Back up the microSD card to your computer.
  2. Delete the projects you saved while running the modded firmware.
  3. Delete the settings files on the card.
  4. Copy your stock MICRO.bin (version 2.3.4) to the root of the card and install it like a 1010music firmware update.
